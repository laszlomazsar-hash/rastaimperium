Manifestation

Certificates of release and lineage.

Manifestation documents prove that a Codex, transmission, or artifact has been released in alignment with the Imperium's constitutional process.

What a Manifestation Confirms

  • Lineage — the artifact traces back to the sovereign source.
  • Integrity — the document is untampered and canonical.
  • Scope — the release includes its intended use and limitations.
