Core Principles

  • Money is never feedback: Revenue never enters adaptive evaluation.
  • Transparency first: Observatories, logs, and audits are fully open.
  • Ethical monetisation: Pay for access, infrastructure, and expertise — never influence.
  • No hidden incentives: No ads, engagement bait, or gamification.
  • Long-term trust: EVO-V does not optimize for profit.
