Institutional Pilots

Prove the system before it scales.

Pilot programs are scoped for regulated and high-accountability environments where replay, traceability, and governance assurance are required before operational adoption.

Read Limitations firstProof RegistryContact engagement desk

What a pilot can include

  • ·Scoped pilot design against a defined governance problem
  • ·Mapping of claims to public evidence and limitations
  • ·Access to Challenge Lab fixtures and auditor handoff path
  • ·Written boundary of what is and is not in scope

What is not offered here

  • ·Production runtime guarantees without sealed artifacts
  • ·Blanket certification or “court-ready” claims without evidence
  • ·Open-ended SaaS deployment without institutional scope
  • ·Performance benchmarks labelled VERIFIED without public capsules

Institutional journey

  1. 01 · Problem

    Governance risk

    Autonomous systems that cannot be reconstructed, challenged, or constrained create institutional liability.

  2. 02 · Method

    Deterministic constitutional control

    EVO-V-style governance: ordered events, version bundles, legal transition matrices, and append-only lineage.

  3. 03 · Evidence

    Proof · receipts · replay

    Public claims connect to the Proof Registry and Evidence Explorer. Unavailable artifacts are labelled, not invented.

    Open →

  4. 04 · Challenge

    Adversarial verification

    Safe deterministic challenges exercise illegal transitions, replay mismatch, and receipt integrity.

    Open →

  5. 05 · Pilot

    Controlled institutional deployment

    Scoped pilots in regulated environments before operational scale-up. Scope and success criteria agreed in writing.

  6. 06 · Assurance

    Ongoing audit / replay / evidence

    Continuous verification surface rather than one-time certification theatre.
