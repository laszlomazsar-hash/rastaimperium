Pilot boundary

Included

  • ·Written evaluation boundary and system under evaluation
  • ·Mapping of institutional concerns to public evidence (L7 capsules)
  • ·Challenge and offline reproduction of supplied sealed artifacts
  • ·Evidence preservation procedure for the evaluator
  • ·Documented limitations and open questions
  • ·Institutional decision ownership remains with the evaluator

Excluded

  • ·Production-wide behaviour or LIVE operational authority
  • ·Full-kernel parity beyond pure-verifier capsule scope
  • ·Certification, conformity, or legal opinion
  • ·Unbounded agent coverage outside the written pilot boundary
  • ·Performance thresholds not established by public evidence

Evidence required

  • · Sealed public artifacts: ART-L7-REPLAY-001, ART-L7-REJECT-001, ART-L7-PARITY-001
  • · Independent offline pure-verifier reproduction records
  • · Challenge inspection where applicable (e.g. CHAL-ILLEGAL-TRANSITION-001)
  • · Explicit list of unresolved gaps (monitoring, telemetry, org governance, legal)
  • · Written success conditions limited to measurable capsule-scoped outcomes

Decision authority: the institution decides whether evidence is sufficient. Rasta Imperium does not issue production approval from this site.

Success conditions

Measurable evaluation outcomes only — not performance guarantees or certification.

  • · Evaluator can obtain and preserve each sealed capsule
  • · Evaluator can run the supplied pure verifier offline and record exit code / digests
  • · Illegal-transition challenge path remains inspectable
  • · Cross-implementation parity report remains inspectable
  • · Evidence boundaries and production authority remain explicitly stated
  • · Unresolved gaps are documented before any scale-up discussion

Decision pathway

Workflow choices for the evaluator — not recommendations issued as product claims.

  • Continue evaluation →

    More public evidence inspection is required before a pilot.

  • Define bounded pilot →

    Current evidence justifies a scoped evaluation activity under institutional controls.

  • Stop →

    Evidence does not currently justify further evaluation.

  • Request clarification →

    Discuss evidence scope, challenge definition, or pilot boundary.

Indicative commercial terms

Scoped, paid, evidence-bound

Final terms are written per engagement. Ranges orient institutional buyers; they are not a public self-serve price list.

Duration

Typically 8–12 weeks (extendable by mutual written agreement).

Indicative investment

Design partner pilots are scoped in the $50k–$150k range depending on stack complexity, governed decision paths, and integration depth. Not a self-serve price list.

You receive

Written boundary, success conditions limited to measurable evaluation outcomes, challenge/reproduction records, evidence handoff notes, and open-question register.

You provide

Decision context, systems in scope, compliance or audit requirements, and a technical counterpart. Institutional judgement remains yours.
