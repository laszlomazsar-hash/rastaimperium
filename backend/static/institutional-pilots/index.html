Design Partner Program

Prove the system before it scales.

Paid, fixed-scope design partner pilots for regulated and high-accountability teams. Replay, traceability, and constitutional bounds are established in writing before any claim of operational adoption.

Apply for design partner pilotProduct pathwayRead Limitations first

What a pilot can include

  • ·Scoped pilot design against a defined governance problem
  • ·Custom constitution mapping for your agent or decision stack
  • ·Mapping of claims to public evidence and limitations
  • ·Access to Challenge Lab fixtures and auditor handoff path
  • ·Written boundary of what is and is not in scope
  • ·Integration support for invariant enforcement on an agreed subset

What is not offered here

  • ·Production runtime guarantees without sealed artifacts
  • ·Blanket certification or “court-ready” claims without evidence
  • ·Open-ended SaaS deployment without institutional scope
  • ·Performance benchmarks labelled VERIFIED without public capsules
  • ·Unlimited agent coverage outside the written pilot boundary

Design partner terms (indicative)

Scoped, paid, evidence-bound

Final commercial terms are written per engagement. The ranges below orient institutional buyers; they are not a public price list for self-serve purchase.

Duration

Typically 8–12 weeks (extendable by mutual written agreement).

Indicative investment

Design partner pilots are scoped in the $50k–$150k range depending on stack complexity, number of governed decision paths, and integration depth.

You receive

Written constitution map, pilot success criteria, challenge results, evidence handoff package, and a production-path recommendation.

You provide

Decision context, systems in scope, compliance or audit requirements, and a technical counterpart for integration windows.

Institutional journey

  1. 01 · Problem

    Governance risk

    Autonomous systems that cannot be reconstructed, challenged, or constrained create institutional liability.

  2. 02 · Method

    Deterministic constitutional control

    EVO-V-style governance: ordered events, version bundles, legal transition matrices, and append-only lineage.

  3. 03 · Evidence

    Proof · receipts · replay

    Public claims connect to the Proof Registry and Evidence Explorer. Unavailable artifacts are labelled, not invented.

    Open →

  4. 04 · Challenge

    Adversarial verification

    Safe deterministic challenges exercise illegal transitions, replay mismatch, and receipt integrity.

    Open →

  5. 05 · Pilot

    Controlled institutional deployment

    Scoped design partner engagement in regulated environments before operational scale-up. Scope and success criteria agreed in writing.

  6. 06 · Assurance

    Ongoing audit / replay / evidence

    Continuous verification surface rather than one-time certification theatre.
