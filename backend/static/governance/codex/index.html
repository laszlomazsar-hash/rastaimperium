The Codex

Rasta Imperium — Constitutional AI Governance — A codex-driven governance platform with dashboards, consulting, enterprise workspaces, and subscription revenue.

The Rastafarai Codex is the constitutional backbone of the architecture. Seven articles define the accountable boundaries for governed autonomous action.

Article I — Containment

Safe boundaries and mutation quarantine

Article II — Observability

100% trace coverage across all layers

Article III — Interruptibility

Human-in-loop and auto-rollback gates

Article IV — Accountability

Immutable audit trails and attribution

Article V — Legitimacy

Governance rituals and deployment workflows

Article VI — Alignment

Value coherence and drift detection

Article VII — Temporal Asymmetry

Long-term stewardship over short-term optimization
