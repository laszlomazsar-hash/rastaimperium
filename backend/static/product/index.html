Product · commercial pathway

Governance you can inspect.Pilots you can buy.

This site is the public constitutional and verification layer — not the execution runtime and not a self-serve SaaS storefront. The commercial path starts with evidence and scoped design partner pilots for teams that cannot allow autonomous systems to fail without a reconstructible record.

Why institutions engage

Reduce liability from opaque autonomy

Boards, CISOs, and regulated operators face growing exposure when AI agents act without reconstructible records or enforceable bounds. The commercial path below is designed for that constraint — not for general-purpose model monitoring.

Reconstructible decisions

When an autonomous system acts, institutions need a clear record of what entered, which rules applied, and what changed. Deterministic replay turns opaque outcomes into inspectable history.

Bounded autonomy

High-consequence agents should operate inside explicit constitutional limits rather than relying on retrospective interpretation or hidden intent.

Audit-ready evidence

Sealed capsules, challenge fixtures, and append-only lineage give auditors and boards a concrete surface to examine instead of marketing claims.

Scoped risk reduction

Pilots establish written boundaries and success criteria before scale-up, reducing institutional liability from ungoverned agent behaviour.

Capability surface

What buyers and auditors can evaluate

Capabilities below are either publicly challengeable on this surface or delivered as pilot / runtime work product under written scope. Performance claims remain UNAVAILABLE until sealed benchmark capsules exist.

Deterministic replay

Identical inputs and event order yield identical terminal state, receipt, and ledger head hashes for sealed capsules.

Illegal-transition rejection

FSM-governed lifecycle edges reject unauthorized state mutations with sealed rejection receipts.

Append-only lineage

Hash-linked audit chronology designed for reconstructibility under institutional review.

Challenge battery

Adversarial fixtures for replay mismatch, altered receipts, and illegal edges — available on the public verification surface.

Constitution mapping

Pilot work product: explicit policy boundaries mapped onto your agent or decision paths before scale-up.
