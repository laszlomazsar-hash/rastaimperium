Closed-loop binding

The Articles interlock. Containment limits the space; Observability and Accountability make every move inspectable; Interruptibility and Reversibility keep recovery possible; Proportionality and Temporal Asymmetry prevent haste and excess. The design target that binds them is the Lyapunov Vow.
