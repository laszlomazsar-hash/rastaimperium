# Article II — Observability

Observability declares that every governance-relevant action must be traceable.
If a decision cannot be reconstructed, it cannot be trusted in constitutional operation.

## Constitutional intent

This article ensures decisions remain legible across infrastructure, data, model behavior, and human intervention.
Trace coverage is a governance requirement, not an optional engineering enhancement.

## Operational controls

- End-to-end traces connect user intent, policy evaluation, and system output.
- Governance dashboards expose coherence, drift, and exception trends.
- Replay tooling allows investigators to reconstruct edge-case decisions.
- Alerting thresholds are versioned alongside policy changes.

## Stewardship note

Observability transforms uncertainty into accountable evidence.
Evidence is what allows a living constitution to evolve without losing legitimacy.
