# Article IV — Accountability

Accountability requires each governance action to carry authorship, provenance, and tamper-evident lineage.
Attribution is mandatory for both human and automated actors.

## Constitutional intent

This article makes responsibility explicit so review, remediation, and trust can scale together.
It prevents governance theater by demanding verifiable records.

## Operational controls

- Signed change events attach actor identity and justification.
- Immutable logs preserve event ordering for forensic analysis.
- Provenance hashes secure document and policy integrity.
- Audit pathways are queryable by compliance and operations teams.

## Stewardship note

Accountability is the memory system of constitutional AI.
Without memory, governance becomes anecdotal and fragile.
