# Article I — Containment

Containment defines the sacred boundary between experimental mutation and production trust.
Every model variation must begin inside a quarantine lane where blast radius, data reach, and external side effects are all bounded by policy.
No artifact can pass into public interfaces until the quarantine lane has completed integrity checks.

## Constitutional intent

This article exists to prevent silent drift, unbounded autonomy, and accidental harm.
Containment keeps the evolutionary engine productive while preserving reversibility and human authority.

## Operational controls

- All policy mutations are first deployed to isolated sandboxes.
- Data access is reduced to the minimum slices needed for verification.
- Promotion gates require cryptographic audit records and signed reviewer approval.
- Emergency rollback paths remain active at every stage.

## Stewardship note

Containment is not anti-innovation.
It is the vessel that lets innovation scale without dissolving accountability.
