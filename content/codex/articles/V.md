# Article V — Legitimacy

Legitimacy defines how governance authority is earned, renewed, and recognized across deployments.
It links policy operation to transparent rituals that stakeholders can inspect.

## Constitutional intent

This article ensures that constitutional controls are accepted because they are visible, consistent, and reviewable.
Legitimacy converts technical governance into social trust.

## Operational controls

- Release rituals include preflight checks, reviewer attestations, and publication logs.
- Exception handling follows publicly documented escalation paths.
- Governance decisions are communicated with rationale and impact notes.
- Renewal cycles revisit assumptions with cross-functional review.

## Stewardship note

Legitimacy is continuous work.
A system that is merely effective but opaque will eventually lose its constitutional mandate.
