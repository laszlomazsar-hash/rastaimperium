# Rasta Imperium Symbolism Layer v1.5

## Purpose
This layer formalizes authentic Rastafari symbolism across the website ecosystem with canonical, respectful usage.

## 1) Canonical symbols
- **Lion of Judah (primary):** Haile Selassie I lineage, courage, sovereignty, resistance.
- **Rasta colors:** red (struggle), gold (royalty/wealth), green (life/Ethiopia), black (depth/identity), white (clarity).
- **Dreadlocks / Lion's mane:** natural covenant, independence, strength.
- **Nyabinghi / drum:** heartbeat rhythm and communal grounding.
- **Ethiopia / Zion:** spiritual homeland and directional coherence.
- **Tree of Life / spiral motifs:** roots-up growth and causal continuity.
